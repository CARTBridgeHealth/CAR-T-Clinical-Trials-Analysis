June 22, 2026 — A Global First

China's NMPA approved Satricabtagene autoleucel (Satri-cel) — the world's first CAR-T therapy targeting a solid tumor (CLDN18.2+ gastric and gastroesophageal junction cancer). This breakthrough is not yet approved in the USA, Europe, or Canada. For patients with advanced gastric cancer — a disease with significantly higher incidence in Chinese populations — China is now the only country offering this option.
