Case Study 案例分析

CAR-T therapy explained — one patient's story CAR-T 疗法详解——一位患者的亲身经历

China is attracting overseas cancer patients with CAR-T therapy because it can be substantially cheaper and faster than options available in Australia, the USA, and the UK. The following case highlights one patient's successful experience — while noting that long-term outcome data and China's status as a medical-tourism hub are still developing. 中国正以更低廉、更快速的 CAR-T 疗法吸引来自澳大利亚、美国和英国的海外癌症患者。以下案例展示了一位患者的成功经历，同时也指出中国长期疗效数据和医疗旅游地位仍在发展中。

Why patients travel 患者为何赴华

Amanda Harvey, an Australian doctor with relapsed multiple myeloma, could not access CAR-T in Australia in 2025. Treatment in the US or UK would have cost ~AUD 1 million. She paid ~AUD 110,000 in China — roughly ten times less — plus travel and living expenses.

澳大利亚医生 Amanda Harvey 患有复发性多发性骨髓瘤，2025 年在澳大利亚无法获得 CAR-T 疗法。在美国或英国治疗费用约为 100 万澳元，而她在中国仅花费约 11 万澳元，约为前者的十分之一。

How it works & what happened 治疗过程与结果

Doctors collected her T cells, modified them to recognize myeloma cells, then infused them back. In Shanghai, she underwent testing, cell collection, chemotherapy, and reinfusion. She felt very ill the first week — but a bone-marrow test two weeks later found no myeloma cells. She then returned to Australia for follow-up care.

医生采集她的 T 细胞，改造使其能识别骨髓瘤细胞，再回输体内。在上海完成检测、细胞采集、化疗和回输后，她第一周感觉非常不适——但两周后骨髓检测显示骨髓瘤细胞完全消失，随后返回澳大利亚继续随访。

Why China is faster 为何中国更快

Her cells were manufactured and returned from Beijing in just 8 days. US manufacturing reportedly takes 2–3 months — a delay that can prevent rapidly worsening patients from receiving their engineered cells in time.

她的细胞在北京仅用 8 天完成制造并回输。而美国据报需要 2–3 个月——这一延误可能导致病情迅速恶化的患者无法及时获得工程化细胞治疗。

What is still unknown 尚待明确的问题

Harvey was in complete remission 7 months after treatment, calling the result excellent. However, she noted that the Shanghai treatment differs somewhat from US/European protocols and does not yet have enough long-term data for reliable comparison. CAR-T is generally used after other treatments fail.

Harvey 在治疗 7 个月后达到完全缓解，称结果出色。但她指出，上海的疗法与美欧方案有所不同，目前尚无足够的长期数据进行可靠比较。CAR-T 目前通常在其他疗法失败后使用。

Why China leads 中国领先的原因

Bloomberg health reporter Karoline Kan attributes China's lead to supportive policy, permissive regulation, advanced hospitals, large patient pools, and extensive clinical trials. China's first CAR-T product was approved in 2021 — 4 years after the US — but its trial volume now leads globally, and larger manufacturing capacity reduces costs.

彭博健康记者 Karoline Kan 将中国的领先归因于政策支持、相对宽松的监管、先进医院、庞大患者群体和大量临床试验。中国首个 CAR-T 产品于 2021 年获批，比美国晚 4 年，但目前试验数量全球领先，更大的制造规模也有助于降低成本。

Medical tourism outlook 医疗旅游前景

China has promoted medical tourism for years but still faces obstacles. Growing pharmaceutical capabilities and social media are improving its reputation. Harvey describes Chinese hospitals as medically efficient but less likely than Australian hospitals to provide bedside support, counseling, or social-work services — a gap our coordination service directly addresses.

中国多年来积极推动医疗旅游，但仍面临障碍。不断增长的医药实力和社交媒体正在提升其声誉。Harvey 认为中国医院医疗效率高，但在床边陪护、心理咨询和社工支持方面不如澳大利亚医院——而这正是我们协调服务直接填补的空白。

June 22, 2026 — A World First

2026 年 6 月 22 日 — 全球首创

China's NMPA approved Satricabtagene autoleucel (Satri-cel) — the world's first CAR-T therapy for solid tumors, targeting CLDN18.2+ gastric cancer. This breakthrough is unavailable in the USA, Canada, or Europe. For patients with advanced gastric cancer — significantly more common in Chinese populations — China is the only option.

中国 NMPA 批准了 Satricabtagene autoleucel（Satri-cel）—— 全球首个针对实体瘤的 CAR-T 疗法，用于 CLDN18.2 阳性胃癌/胃食管交界腺癌。这一突破在美国、加拿大和欧洲均无法获得。对于晚期胃癌患者（在华人群体中发病率显著更高），中国是唯一的选择。
