What is 奕凯达®?

什么是奕凯达®？

奕凯达® is a CAR-T cell therapy that uses your own immune cells (T cells) to fight B-cell lymphoma. Doctors collect your T cells, genetically modify them to recognize and destroy cancer cells, then infuse them back into your body. It is manufactured by Fosun Kite — a joint venture between Fosun Pharma and Kite/Gilead — and is the same product as Yescarta sold in the USA and Europe.

奕凯达®是一种CAR-T细胞疗法，利用您自身的免疫细胞（T细胞）来对抗B细胞淋巴瘤。医生采集您的T细胞，对其进行基因改造使其能够识别并消灭癌细胞，然后将改造后的细胞回输至您体内。该产品由复星医药与吉利德/Kite合资的复星凯特生产，与美国和欧洲销售的Yescarta为同款产品。

What is 恺力美®?

什么是恺力美®？

恺力美® targets Claudin 18.2 (CLDN18.2), a protein found on the surface of gastric cancer cells. It is developed by CARsgen Therapeutics and represents a historic milestone — the first time CAR-T technology has successfully treated a solid tumor. Results were published in The Lancet in 2025.

恺力美®靶向Claudin 18.2（CLDN18.2）蛋白，该蛋白表达于胃癌细胞表面。该产品由科济药业开发，是一项历史性突破——CAR-T技术首次成功应用于实体瘤治疗。相关研究结果于2025年发表在《柳叶刀》杂志。
